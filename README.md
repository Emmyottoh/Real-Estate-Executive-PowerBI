# Executive Real Estate Performance & Revenue Analytics Suite

## Project Overview
Real estate executives require high-level commercial visibility and granular market benchmarking to evaluate property sales performance, monitor total revenue trends, and identify top-performing geographic markets. This project delivers a multi-page interactive Power BI analytics suite engineered to process and visualize 1,000,000+ transaction records across diverse property categories.

![Executive Real Estate Analytics Dashboard](Executive%20Real%20Estate%20Analytics%20Dashboard-BI.png)

## Technical Stack & Tools
* **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Power BI Desktop, Interactive Dashboard Design, Advanced DAX (Calculated Columns & Measures)
* **Data Engineering & ETL:** Power Query, Data Cleaning, Normalization, Data Quality Auditing
* **Data Modeling & Architecture:** Relational Star Schema, Date Hierarchy Alignment
* **Core Competencies:** Analytical Skills, Statistics, Data Analytics, Revenue Benchmarking, Executive Communication

## Key Features & Business Impact
* **Large-Scale Data Architecture:** Engineered robust ETL pipelines to ingest, clean, and normalize 1,000,000+ real estate transaction records, resolving legacy timeline default errors and date hierarchy mismatches.
* **Executive Revenue Tracking:** Built an executive summary dashboard monitoring ₦418 Billion in total sales revenue and 1.05 Million properties sold.
* **Granular Market Benchmarking:** Designed town-level price benchmarks and an interactive Town vs. Category revenue matrix to isolate high-value markets (e.g., Greenwich and Stamford).
* **Statistical Sales Analysis:** Applied descriptive **Statistics** and trend modeling to evaluate sales trajectory, price distribution, and volume fluctuations over time.

## Data Pipeline & Methodology
1. **ETL & Data Cleaning:** Applied Power Query transformations within Power BI to correct schema shifts, standardize pricing columns, and remove record anomalies.
2. **Data Modeling:** Built a relational **Data Modeling** structure connecting property transactions, calendar tables, and location lookups with optimized DAX performance.
3. **Interactive Reporting:** Implemented cross-filtering, dynamic slicers, and town benchmarking matrices to facilitate clear **Communication** with executive stakeholders.
